04 / FAQ

Good questions.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

The installer isn't code-signed (certificates cost money), so SmartScreen warns about any new app. Click More info → Run anyway. Every Wispen update is digitally signed, and the app refuses any update that isn't.

How do updates work?

Automatically. Wispen checks for a new version when it starts and every few hours. When one is out, a popup shows what's new: click Update now and it installs and restarts in seconds, keeping your settings and history.

Do I need an account?

No. Wispen works fully without one. You can optionally sign up with your email inside the app to get a profile.

Does it need the internet?

Only for things that are online by nature: web search, YouTube, news and weather. Voice recognition, the AI, your history and screen reading all run offline on your PC.

How do I turn on the local AI?

Install the free Ollama, then run:

ollama pull llama3.2:3b

Wispen detects it automatically. Without it everything else still works, and questions go to web search instead.

What gets downloaded on first launch?

The speech model (~150 MB in Lite, ~500 MB otherwise), a natural voice (~60 MB), and only on PCs with an NVIDIA GPU of 4 GB or more, the GPU speech library (~0.7 GB). Wispen is usable the whole time, using the CPU and the Windows voice until they arrive.
